I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Alberta, working with Prof. Wylie Stroberg, where I work at the intersection of mechanical engineering and biophysics. My research focuses on machine-learning–driven modeling of intracellular transport, with a primary emphasis on how endoplasmic-reticulum (ER) crowding and heterogeneity shape protein trafficking. My work spans from formulating theory and implementing algorithms to running large-scale simulations, and leveraging operator-learning and auxiliary-variable approaches (Mori–Zwanzig/GLE) to extract mechanistic, clinically relevant insights. I earned a double degree B.Sc in Mechanical Engineering and Aerospace Engineering from Sharif University of Technology, my M.Sc in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Alberta, and completed my Ph.D. at the University of Toronto (UofT), working with Prof. Chul B. Park.
